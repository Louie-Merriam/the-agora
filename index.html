About the Colloquium

The Agora is a student-run virtual conference held on Zoom from October 24–25, 2026. The colloquium is dedicated towards showcasing original research by youth scholars in Classics, ancient history, late antiquity, medieval studies, and the reception of the classical tradition.

The Agora offers a unique opportunity to share your work with other capable students, discuss classics-related topics with like-minded peers, and even win an award. Whether you apply as a Presenter or a Discussion Participant, you will contribute to rich, substantive conversations.

Format & Schedule

The conference will open with a short keynote address from a distinguished Classics professor, followed by student presentations and moderated discussions.

Selected presenters will have 5–10 minutes to deliver their paper, followed by a moderator-led Q&A using their pre-submitted questions, and finally a broader discussion with the audience. We hope that this interactive format will deepen your engagement with the Classics, connect you with other students, and help you develop skills in both presenting and participating in academic dialogue.

Call for Papers

We invite submissions of 250-word abstracts on any topic related to:

  • Greek and Roman history, literature, or philosophy
  • Late Antique and Byzantine studies
  • Classical afterlives and reception history
  • Art, archaeology, and material culture
  • Interdisciplinary approaches bridging Classics with STEM, Political Science, and other fields

We will review submissions and accept students based on the quality and relevance of the proposals.

Submission Guidelines

  • A 250-word abstract summarizing your research argument and evidence
  • A short note (50 words) on the questions or topics you would like to explore
